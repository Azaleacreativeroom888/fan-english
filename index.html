Why This Diner Exists

Two reasons, one site. First: Japanese manga deserves to be read by English speakers around the world — and good translation is how that happens. Second: the best way to learn English is through things you actually love. This site is built on both of those beliefs. No textbooks. No filler. Just real language, real culture, and real manga.
